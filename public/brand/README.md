# Ink & Iron — Option 2 assets

These production assets are extracted directly from the approved Option 2 reference image. The symbol, copper spark, proportions, surface tone, and wordmark are preserved from that source rather than redrawn.

## Primary assets

- `ink-and-iron-mark-soft-background.png` — standalone mark on the original warm paper background, 1600 × 1600.
- `ink-and-iron-mark-transparent.png` — standalone mark with alpha transparency, 1600 × 1600.
- `ink-and-iron-lockup-soft-background.png` — mark plus `Ink & Iron` wordmark on the original warm paper background, 2500 × 700.
- `ink-and-iron-lockup-transparent.png` — mark plus `Ink & Iron` wordmark with alpha transparency, 2500 × 700.
- `ink-and-iron-favicon-master.png` — 512 × 512 favicon master.

## Favicon exports

The directory includes PNG exports at 16, 32, 48, 180, and 256 pixels, plus a self-contained `favicon.svg`. Standard browser copies also live at the root of `public/`, including `favicon.ico` and `apple-touch-icon.png`.

## Source

`ink-and-iron-option2-source.png` is the approved reference retained as the source of truth for future exports.
